What we do

Seven jobs we take off your desk.

Pick the ones you need. Most clients start with one and hand over more once they see the books settle.

  1. 01

    Bank reconciliation

    Bank, credit card and accounting records matched to the cent — daily, weekly or monthly. Errors surface early instead of at tax time.

  2. 02

    Payroll

    Wages, deductions, super and statutory obligations, processed on schedule. Your staff get paid correctly and on the day they expect.

  3. 03

    Accounts payable

    Invoices checked, approved and paid on time. No duplicate payments, no supplier chasing you, no late fees.

  4. 04

    Accounts receivable

    Invoices out the door, payments tracked, polite follow-up on what's overdue. Cash arrives sooner.

  5. 05

    STP reconciliation

    Payroll data cross-checked against ATO records, discrepancies fixed and year-end finalised properly.

  6. 06

    Month-end workings & reporting

    Reconciliations, expense review and financial statements — verified, compliant and in your inbox on time.

  7. 07

    Backlog catch-up

    Months or years behind? We work through the gaps, bring every record up to date and hand you back a clean, tax-ready set of books.
